Fictional hillclimb circuit on a farm near Fish Creek, South Gippsland. rFactor scratch build by legendsatlunch, converted to GTL. Also installed at this venue: CIRC Sprint.

Drawn from the AI waypoint file: road centre, road width, pit lane and grid. Allroad opens extra side roads that the AI line doesn't use, so they don't appear here.
